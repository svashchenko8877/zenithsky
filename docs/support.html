Zenith Sky – Support

The sky looks wrong or points the wrong way

Allow Location and Motion access in Settings → Zenith Sky, and make sure Compass Calibration is on (Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → System Services). Move your phone in a figure-eight to calibrate. You can also drag the sky with a finger at any time (tap the hand icon).

Wrong times or place

Times use your phone's time zone and your current location. If Location is off, the app uses a default location until you enable it.

Restore my Pro purchase

Open the lock icon or Settings (sliders icon) → Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple ID used to buy.
