Zenith Sky – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Data we collect

Zenith Sky does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it makes no network requests of its own.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your approximate location only to calculate what is above your horizon (rise and set times, the position of stars, planets and the Moon). Your coordinates stay on your device. To show a place name, iOS may send your location to Apple's geocoding service; that request is made by the operating system, not by us, and we never receive it.

Motion sensors

The gyroscope and compass are used on-device so the sky moves as you point your phone. Sensor data is never stored or sent anywhere.

In-app purchase

The optional "Zenith Pro" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details or personal information.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
