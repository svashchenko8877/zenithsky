Zenith Sky

Your live night sky: stars, constellations, planets, the Moon and deep-sky objects, with a Moon phase calendar, a nightly planner and eclipse and meteor-shower dates. Works offline.

  • Point your phone at the sky and the map follows you
  • Tap any object for brightness, position, rise and set times, then follow the arrow to find it
  • Tonight: Moon phase, darkness window and which planets are up
  • Moon calendar with phases, moonrise, moonset and supermoons
  • Meteor showers and eclipses
  • Red night mode, no account, no ads, no tracking
